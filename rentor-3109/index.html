3109 H STREET · DESK MANUAL

HOW THE DAY WORKS

Daylight lasts five minutes. Finish the stops, then get back inside the office before dark.

The route

Work the list

Earn the rent. The card at the top names the next stop. The gold pin on the corner map is that door, and a line runs from you to it. North is the top of the map. 3109 is the south edge.

H Street ends at a circle in Ferndale. That is not a place to wait. Turn around. 3109 is south.

The stops

Go inside

Step up to the door and go in. Some homes are ordinary. Some are packed to the ceiling. Look around, then talk. The person stands in the center of the screen. Their answer stays on that card. The street has its own noise, and a talk is spoken.

Ask for the rent first. If they will not pay, grab them, walk them outside, and throw them into the street. P opens the phone. Dial 444-0191. The cops put them in the wagon and take them to debtors' jail.

A missed month cools them off. Another one and they leave, and the unit sits vacant until you place someone new.

The desk

Answer, then drive

The question at the door is the work. A sound answer keeps the home. A costly one comes out of the drawer.

Henry's desk opens the north listings. One stop is an owner who still runs the building alone. Listen before you name a fee. The signature is a management agreement.

The company car is out front. Get in, and get out the same way. Any empty car on the street can be taken. The far end of H Street is a drive.

The clock starts when the office opens, and it keeps running while you read a note. Up to 24 people can share the street.
