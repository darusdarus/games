Start on Trinity Street in Myrtletown. The road turns through Redwood Acres, up the Redwood Sky Walk, through Sequoia Park Zoo and the tree walk, into the Carson Mansion, then a zipline down to the slough bridge.
Safe rooms at the fair hall, the zoo, 3109 H Street, the mansion, and the Samoa Cookhouse. The channel span is long and high. If it goes, row the skiff — four oars, W rows, A and D turn.
